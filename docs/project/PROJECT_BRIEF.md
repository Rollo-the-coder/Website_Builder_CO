# Project Brief

## Project Name

Personal Website / Digital Systems Business Site

Working brand name: **Gotta Build**

## Client / Business

Erik personal/business website for a website + digital systems business (Gotta Build).

Public contact: erik@gotta.build


## Niche

Website creation and digital systems for small businesses:

- Modern website builds and rebuilds
- Narrative/messaging and offer clarity
- Lead capture forms, bookings, and payments
- Portals, dashboards, and operations workflows
- AI-enabled automations, chatbots/agents, and AI SEO/AEO support
- Launch hardening, analytics, and ongoing management

## Location

Seattle / Bellevue / Eastside first for trust and acquisition.

Remote-capable nationally/online after initial local traction.

## Primary Goal

Build a slick, modern client-acquisition website that functions as:

1. Sales page
2. Portfolio/proof piece
3. Narrative positioning piece
4. Services explainer
5. Boost Baseball case study hub
6. Client intake and website/system audit request funnel
7. Foundation for future payment/deposit flow

This website must position the offer as website + digital systems transformation, not only "website design."

## Target Audience

Primary audience: small business owners/operators who need better websites and digital systems.

Priority niches:

- Youth sports academies
- Baseball training businesses
- Gyms and coaches
- Tutors and education businesses
- Small local service businesses
- Clinics and wellness businesses
- International schools and education programs

## Main Offer

Core framing:

"I help businesses turn messy websites and manual workflows into clear digital systems that generate leads, handle signups, collect payments, automate communication, and make operations easier."

Framework:

- Clarify: messaging, narrative, offer structure, CTA flow
- Build: websites, forms, bookings, payments, portals, dashboards
- Manage: updates, analytics, security checks, automations, AI support

Business model:

- Setup/build project fees
- Ongoing monthly management retainers

Primary CTA: Request a Website/System Audit

Secondary CTA: View Boost Case Study

## Constraints

- Launch v1 quickly to support real outreach and sales conversations.
- Do not overbuild before proof of traction.
- Keep scope practical: one flagship case study (Boost Baseball) first.
- Do not present as a generic freelance portfolio or commodity "website builder."
- Maintain grounded small-business clarity (not abstract AI-startup positioning).
- Brand name, legal name, contact details, and many assets are TBD.
- Pricing should use starting prices (Launch $1,250 / Business+Lead $2,500 / Operations $4,500+) and clarify custom quotes depend on scope.
- Founding client offer: three qualified Seattle/Eastside slots; track manually via `site.foundingSlotsRemaining`.
- Fit-call requests use the contact form intent `fit_call` (no public calendar yet). Optional `NEXT_PUBLIC_FIT_CALL_URL` reserved for a later success-state booking link.
- Style switcher lives on `/design-lab`, not in the primary conversion path.

## Definition Of Done

Project is done for v1 when:

- A credible, polished site is live and usable for outreach/sales conversations.
- Homepage clearly communicates "websites + digital systems" positioning.
- Services and process are understandable without sales-call hand-holding.
- Boost Baseball is published as a flagship case study with practical proof narrative.
- Audit request CTA and form funnel work end-to-end (destination email configured).
- Package/pricing guidance exists with realistic starting ranges.
- Mobile-first layout, accessible defaults, and SEO metadata are in place.
- Legal pages are present for launch requirements.
