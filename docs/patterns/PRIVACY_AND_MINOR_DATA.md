# Privacy And Minor Data

Collect the minimum data needed. Document what you collect, who can access it, and how long you keep it.

## Baseline Practices

- Collect only data required for the feature or business need
- Document what personal data is collected and why
- Document who can access it (roles, admins, integrations)
- Document retention and deletion expectations
- Keep privacy, cookie, terms, and refund docs aligned with actual behavior

## Extra Care For Sensitive Contexts

Be especially careful with:

- Children or minor-related data
- Parent or guardian contact information
- Schedules, rosters, photos, and location details
- Health, education, or payment-related personal data

## Guidance

- Do not assume legal compliance — flag legal review where child/minor data, payments, health, education, or sensitive personal data exists
- Avoid minors creating accounts directly unless intentionally designed and legally reviewed
- Do not store sensitive data in logs, email bodies, or public fields
- Provide a path to honor deletion or export requests where applicable
